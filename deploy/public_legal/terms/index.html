Conditions for the private use of APEX Intelligence Core and its authorised bank connections.

Last updated: 12 November 2026  ·  Application: APEX Intelligence Core  ·  United Kingdom

Private use only. APEX Intelligence Core is a private, owner-operated research and personal financial analysis application. It is not offered as a public financial service.

1. Permitted use

The application is intended for the registered owner to access and analyse only financial information that they own or are expressly authorised to access. The owner must comply with applicable bank and Enable Banking consent conditions and avoid use for third parties without the necessary permissions and agreements.

2. Banking connectivity

Bank connections are provided through Enable Banking and participating financial institutions, subject to their separate terms, availability and consent requirements. Account information will be accessible only after the required authorisation is completed. APEX does not request or store online banking passwords.

3. No payment or investment service

Account summaries, categorisations, forecasts and automated observations are information tools, not financial, legal, investment or tax advice. APEX is not a bank and does not guarantee that account information is complete, continuously available or free of delays. Unless separately authorised and enabled, the application does not initiate bank payments or trades.

4. Security and automated actions

APEX is designed to restrict sensitive operations to authorised access. Any automated action that changes external accounts, initiates a transaction or shares sensitive information must satisfy the application's governance and approval controls. These controls must be verified in the deployed system before enabling live actions.

5. Service availability

As a privately operated application, APEX may be unavailable during maintenance, provider outages, revoked permissions or development. No public service-level commitment or uptime guarantee is offered.

6. Data protection

The accompanying Privacy Policy explains intended processing of account information, account permissions and records. The owner can withdraw banking consent using the bank or Enable Banking's consent interface.

7. Contact and changes

For matters involving the registered application, use the data-protection email submitted in the Enable Banking application registration. These terms may be updated when the owner adds or changes authorised integrations. Last updated: 12 November 2026.
