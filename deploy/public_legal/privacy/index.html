How APEX handles bank-account information in its private, owner-authorised application.

Last updated: 12 November 2026  ·  Application: APEX Intelligence Core  ·  United Kingdom

Scope. APEX Intelligence Core (“APEX”) is a privately operated, non-commercial application intended for use solely by its owner with bank accounts the owner has personally authorised. It is not a public banking, payment, lending, or investment service.

This notice describes the intended processing when an account connection is enabled. Registering the application does not itself mean bank data is already being accessed.

1. Who operates the application

APEX is operated by the individual who registered the APEX Intelligence Core application with Enable Banking, for that individual's personal use. For privacy queries, use the data-protection contact email provided during the application's Enable Banking registration and shown to the account holder in the Enable Banking consent record. This page does not publish the owner's personal contact details.

2. Information the application may process

  • Bank information: account identifiers, account names, currency, balances, transaction dates, amounts, merchant or reference descriptions and other fields authorised by the bank and made available through Enable Banking.
  • Connection credentials: application identifiers, cryptographic signing credentials, session identifiers, consent status and expiration dates. Bank passwords are not requested or stored by APEX.
  • Operational information: timestamps, connection status, error codes and security/audit records needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.
  • User-generated analysis: personally requested budget summaries, transaction categories and financial insights, if and when those features are activated.

3. Why information is used

Information is used only to connect the owner's authorised accounts, display and analyse account information, support personal budgeting and financial planning, maintain connection security, prevent misuse and resolve technical problems. Bank information is not collected for advertising or sale.

4. Account authorisation and permissions

A connection is initiated only when the account holder chooses to authorise it through the relevant bank and Enable Banking flow. The requested access depends on that bank and the permissions selected during consent. An authorisation may expire or be revoked; reauthorisation may then be necessary. APEX cannot extend a bank's consent period unilaterally.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Bank data may pass through the authorised financial institution and Enable Banking to provide account-information access. Hosting, connectivity or security providers used by the deployed version of APEX may process information necessary to deliver the service. If an AI or analytics provider is subsequently enabled to process financial information, that processing must be disclosed and configured before financial data is sent to it. APEX is not intended to make bank data publicly available.

6. Security

Access to APEX is intended to be restricted to the authorised owner. The operational deployment must protect private keys, session data and account records with appropriate access controls, transport encryption, protected secret storage and audit logging. No website or network service can guarantee absolute security. The final production controls must be verified before live financial data is processed.

7. Retention and deletion

Bank information is intended to be kept only for the period necessary for the owner's authorised analysis and personal records. When the owner removes a connection or requests deletion, the system should delete stored account information unless a specific legal obligation or narrowly necessary security record requires longer retention. Operational logs and backups may follow separately configured retention periods. Those periods and the actual deletion workflow must be verified against the deployed system before live processing begins.

8. International processing

The bank, Enable Banking and any enabled infrastructure providers may operate in different countries. Any transfer of personal data must be assessed under applicable law, and relevant provider privacy information and safeguards should be consulted. This notice does not assert that all processing remains within the United Kingdom.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

The account holder may stop using APEX, withdraw banking access in the applicable bank/Enable Banking interface, and request access to or removal of locally stored data. Where UK GDPR applies, statutory rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, subject to applicable exceptions. Personal or household processing can be subject to different legal rules depending on actual use.

Banking consents may also be reviewed and withdrawn through Enable Banking's data-sharing consent interface. UK privacy concerns may be referred to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Cookies and tracking

These static public legal pages do not themselves set analytics or advertising cookies. The protected APEX console and any external providers may use essential authentication or security cookies under their own notices and configurations.

11. Changes to this notice

This notice may be revised as integrations are activated or the service changes. Material changes affecting how banking information is used should be made available before the relevant processing begins. The latest version date appears above.
