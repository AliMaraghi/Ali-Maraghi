## Thesis and counterproposal

**Thesis:** Leaving a place does not immediately change the invisible habits that
organise attention. The coast offers a chance to notice those habits, rather than
an escape promised by travel imagery. A repeated doorway, horizon and footstep
become the film's visual grammar: boundary, distance, return.

**Counterproposal:** A film without narration may say this more honestly. Prepare
an ambient-only version from the same edit for founder review. Neither version
should imply a life event or emotion the founder has not confirmed.

**Creative constraint:** Resist a destination montage. Hold after movement ends.
Use changes in ambient sound to carry cuts. Avoid stock superlatives, inspirational
commands, synthetic memories, imitation of a named filmmaker, and a generic music
build. Originality and audience response remain untested.

## Editing, audio and export plan

1. Create an inventory and proxy edit from authorised copies. Inspect codec, frame
   rate, orientation, duration and recorded audio before choosing project settings.
2. Assemble the 75-second silent picture cut and compare against the narration cut.
3. Use source-matched frame rate, restrained exposure/white-balance adjustments,
   and consistent black levels. Do not fabricate scenic features or personal events.
4. Prefer usable original sound. Check for noise, clipping, intelligibility and
   accidental music. Any score is optional and requires a documented licence.
5. Produce accurate SRT subtitles from the approved recorded words, with no subtitle
   over the deliberate silent ending. Inspect timing and readability manually.
6. Draft export: 1920×1080, 16:9, source-matched frame rate, 48 kHz audio. Final codec,
   peak/loudness and platform-specific deliverables are chosen after tool and media
   inspection. No render command is run at this stage.
7. Recompose a 9:16 version shot by shot, and produce a distinct 20–30 second teaser
   only after the full film passes review. Automated centre cropping is not approval.
