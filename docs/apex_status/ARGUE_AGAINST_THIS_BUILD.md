# Case against this build

The strongest alternative is a conventional checklist, a small evidence folder,
and a direct assistant session when reasoning is needed. That avoids a second
runtime, specialist coordination, connectors and long-term maintenance. The
current full conventional policy checklist matches APEX 24/24 on the frozen
synthetic cases. No measured ChatGPT/Claude/Codex or human comparison exists;
no claim about their current product features, prices or relative intelligence
is made. APEX must demonstrate durable provenance/replay saves correction effort
before more architecture is justified.

Positive results can be explained by developer-authored fixtures and repeated
exposure to the benchmark. The same implementer wrote and reviewed the adaptation.
The kit's 69 green tests missed five reproducible defects. This is a reason to
require independent cases, not to treat this self-review as independent assurance.

Important limits:

- OWP plans queries but retrieves nothing. Snapshot hashes prove retained bytes,
  not source truth, inspection, completeness, independence or customer demand.
- Hypotheses are templates. Three causal labels do not prove substantive novelty.
- Local permission labels do not authenticate an owner or stop a same-user Python
  caller. The SQLite chain can be rewritten by a sufficiently privileged attacker.
- Workflow timing is checked after a bounded stage; it is not preemptive timeout.
  History scans and a single transaction will need redesign before large workloads.
- More files/roles expand maintenance and test burden; no independently measured
  income, time saving, original artistic signature or production uptime exists.
- Missing lawful connectors, approved footage, model funding and independent
  reviewers block real outcomes. Private data cannot safely enter this prototype.

The smallest falsification experiment: an independent reviewer supplies new
public evidence packets for one narrow decision class. Compare manual checklist
and APEX with identical inputs/time/access, measure unsupported claims, correction
minutes and useful decisions, retain raw outputs. If provenance/replay offers no
measurable benefit, pivot to the simpler tool. No outreach or purchases are needed
for that comparison. A field demand test remains separately authorized.

Decision now: CONTINUE bounded offline validation, not architecture expansion or
release. Nov 30 remains a continue/pivot/stop review, not a completion promise.
