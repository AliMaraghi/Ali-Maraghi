# Marketing funnel — APEX, organic only (£0/month)

Product (owner's answer: "all" → treated as APEX itself, the only real
product this account can describe without inventing one): a local-first
personal AI system that proposes but never auto-sends/auto-spends — every
action needs the owner's own one-click approval.
Budget: £0/month — organic content, SEO, and opt-in email/DM only. No paid
ads, so Instagram/Google Ads spend-based targeting is out of scope; this
funnel is content + SEO + direct opt-in.
Channels available (from the brief, minus paid ads at £0 budget):
Instagram (organic), SEO/blog content, opt-in email/DM sequence.

## S1 — Three personas

### Persona 1 — "Overwhelmed but in control" (Amara, 34, senior analyst, London)
- **Dغdغه اصلی:** her finances/health data are spread across 6 apps, and she doesn't trust any single one of them to act on her behalf without checking first.
- **زبان:** direct, slightly skeptical of "AI" marketing claims, responds to specifics not adjectives.
- **اعتراض اصلی:** "every AI assistant I've tried either does too little or does something I didn't ask for."
- **Trigger:** a concrete example of the system catching its OWN mistake and refusing to act without approval.
- **کانال واقعی:** LinkedIn/Instagram long-form posts (she reads, doesn't scroll-skip, on lunch breaks).
- **چرا این پرسونا:** she represents the real differentiator (human-approval-gated, never auto-acts) better than a generic "busy professional" persona would.

### Persona 2 — "Privacy-first" (Jess, 29, software engineer, Manchester)
- **دغدغه اصلی:** does not want her financial/health data leaving her own machine or being used to train a model.
- **زبان:** technical, reads documentation before trusting a claim, dismisses marketing superlatives instantly.
- **اعتراض اصلی:** "where does my data actually go, and can I verify that, not just be told it."
- **Trigger:** seeing the actual code/architecture (open about what is and isn't local), not a trust badge.
- **کانال واقعی:** a technical blog post / Hacker News / a long-form SEO article she finds searching "local-first AI assistant."
- **چرا این پرسونا:** she will not convert from an ad or a short post at all — SEO/content is the ONLY channel that reaches her, which is exactly what the £0 budget constraint allows.

### Persona 3 — "Burned by over-automation" (Priya, 41, small business owner, Birmingham)
- **دغدغه اصلی:** a past automation tool once sent a client the wrong invoice while she wasn't looking; she now manually checks everything, which is exhausting.
- **زبان:** warm, story-driven, responds to relatable anecdotes more than specs.
- **اعتراض اصلی:** "I got burned once, I'm not letting anything touch my business without me clicking yes."
- **Trigger:** a short story (hers or a stand-in's) about exactly that scenario, with the resolution being "and it would have asked me first."
- **کانال واقعی:** Instagram Reels/carousel posts (short, visual, story-led) shared in small-business owner groups.
- **چرا این پرسونا:** she is reachable organically (small-business Instagram communities are active and un-paywalled) and her objection maps directly to the product's actual safety model.

## S3 — Conversion funnel

1. **Landing page**
   - **Headline:** "An AI assistant that proposes. You decide. Every time."
   - **Subhead:** "Local-first, human-approval-gated — see the actual approval screen before you sign up for anything."
   - **3 benefits:** (1) Nothing acts without your one-click approval, ever. (2) Your data stays on your own machine by default. (3) Every proposal and decision is logged — nothing happens invisibly.
   - **Social proof:** a real screenshot of an actual approval request + commentary (not a fabricated testimonial — none exists yet; use the product's own transparency as the proof instead).
   - **CTA:** "See your first approval screen →" (opt-in form, email only, nothing else requested)
   - **Conversion metric:** visit → opt-in rate.

2. **First email/message (opt-in only)**
   - Confirms signup, sets the expectation ("nothing runs automatically, ever") explicitly, links to a 2-minute walkthrough.
   - **Conversion metric:** open rate, click-through to walkthrough.

3. **Follow-up 1 (opt-in only, day 2-3)**
   - Persona-matched story (reuse the relevant S2 message above based on which persona's content they opted in from).
   - **Conversion metric:** click-through to "connect your first data source."

4. **Follow-up 2 (opt-in only, day 5-7)**
   - Direct ask: "set up your first approval rule" — the actual activation moment, not another story.
   - **Conversion metric:** activation rate (% who complete one real approval).
