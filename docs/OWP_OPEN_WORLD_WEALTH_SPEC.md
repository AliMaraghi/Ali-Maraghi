# OWP / Open-World Wealth Discovery Directive — Non-negotiable Spec

## Origin and exact naming

The founder calls this OWP. The available canonical record explicitly names **Open-World Wealth Discovery Directive** in §7-1 and **Open-World Opportunity Engine** in the annex. Do not invent an older full form for 'OWP' if absent. `Open World Search` is a worldview, not a promise that the internet will be searched without network/data/rights limits.

## Discovery is unbounded in hypothesis SPACE, bounded in EXECUTION

No prefabricated list of eight 'business ideas' can replace open-world search. Search across all lawful domains and geographies: corporate procurement, scientific technology transfer, regulatory shifts, housing and services, location-driven markets, UK public contracts, international tenders, company registries, patents, standards, skills, workforce demands, overlooked customers and many combinations. Discovery programs can broaden axes; actions are always permissioned, budget-limited, risk-bounded and reversible where possible.

### OWP opportunity data model

- Stable `opportunity_id`; date, domain, geographic scope, industry, delivery mechanism, causal demand claim.
- Legal and platform constraints; evidentiary source URLs, publisher, timestamp, license, content digest and independent-group verification.
- Competition and substitute products, customer interviews, observed transactions where lawful, market and demand provenance.
- Founder’s entry rights, skills, available time and capital; founder-established constraints only, never fabricated cash.
- Entry costs, recurring operating costs, acquisition costs, fiscal/regulatory considerations, alternatives and opportunity cost.
- Worst/base/upside scenario, ranges of capture probability, impact and reversibility, with sensitivity and separate *estimated* versus *actual* outcome.
- Adversarial rejection case, hidden counterfactual, comparison with doing nothing and competing already-working solutions.
- Smallest authorised market test: who can be asked, what consent is required, what evidence would falsify demand.
- Owner, follow-up date, verified state, audit trail, approvals, decision, results of actual experiment.

### Open-world discovery algorithm

1. **Expand** question beyond the initial industry/location using search axes (geography, sector, bottleneck, customer problem, regulatory friction, asset, technology, customer type).
2. **Locate** authorised public primary datasets, log their expected access rights, retention and costs; no credential guessing, forbidden scraping, piracy or backdoor access.
3. **Triangulate** candidate claims with genuinely independent observation groups; identical press-release syndications are one source family.
4. **Counterfactual** create mutually exclusive explanations and test designs; forced third-way solution is not automatically better.
5. **Disconfirm** identify all no-demand, bad-terms, capital or regulatory objections BEFORE ranking.
6. **Economics** calculate real currency units with Decimal or equivalent, explicitly display sensitivity and confidence provenance; no multiplications of unjustified certainty scores.
7. **Capture** check ability to execute, time, skill, capital and competition with founder’s actual constraints; do not treat public tender headline value as expected income.
8. **Propose** reversible authorised experiment; never automatically sign, buy, recruit, register a company, submit a bid, contact a lead or spend money.
9. **Observe** real outcomes, track hit/miss and opportunity selection biases, compare to simple retrieval baseline, learn only after independently confirmed results.
10. **Store** versioned records in one auditable registry for cross-session reasoning and delayed follow-up.

### Ranking without self-delusion

Require one dated genuine demand signal, at least one falsifiable capture hypothesis and a realistic delivery model before rank promotion. All scenario inputs can be speculative and must be displayed as assumptions. Even two independently labelled articles are not sufficient to claim customer demand. Economic value may be negative. Calculate downside separately; financial performance metrics in a sandbox do not count as revenue. An opportunity with unknown capital/permissions cannot be marketed as 'zero-cost'.

### Bounded runtime and actual source coverage

Target connectors include UK Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Companies House, Companies House filing API, UK legislation, FCA, SEC EDGAR, arXiv, university innovation disclosures, relevant job boards, investor reports, patent databases and official statistics. Exact endpoint, licence and rate limits are verification tasks for future implementation. The current Python reference intentionally does not call any of these. No background discovery can occur until a lawful connector, queue workers and an owner-approved network policy are built.

### Metrics that should determine continuation

Observed conversion from candidate to verified lead; verified lead to consent-based customer call; actual willingness to pay; contribution margin adjusted for founder time; citation validity; independent source diversity; false positive rate; best-of-N versus retrieval baseline; project hours spent; opportunity decay; compliance violations; net positive return after costs. Use a 21-day test window for a continue/pivot/stop decision, but do not confuse evaluation deadline with a guaranteed fully operational system.

### Anti-shortcut rules

Do not replace OWP with a static 'Opportunity Intelligence' dashboard. Do not remove edge cases to increase pass rate. Do not assume low-capital offers are the only acceptable ideas; capital is a **constraint for execution**, not a filter forbidding research. Do not limit geography without founder direction; do not include a country merely to please the user when genuine suitability evidence is absent. Distinguish public-company news from any confidential employer information.
